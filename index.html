Threadory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Threadory ("the app") is a cross-stitch and embroidery thread inventory and project management app. Your privacy matters to us, and this app was built on a simple principle: your data is yours and it stays on your device.

Data Collection

Threadory does not collect, transmit, store remotely, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Data Storage

All information you enter in the app — your thread inventory, projects, photos, PDF patterns, shopping lists, notes and settings — is stored locally on your device only. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself using the app's export and share features.

Camera Permission

The app requests camera access only for two optional features: scanning thread barcodes and matching colors from photos. Images and barcode data are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. You can use the app fully without granting camera access.

Photos and Files

Photos and PDF files you attach to projects are chosen by you and stored locally within the app's private storage. They are only shared if you use a share/export action yourself.

Backups and Exports

Backup (JSON) and export (CSV) files are generated on your device and saved or shared only to destinations you choose. We have no access to them.

Purchases

Threadory Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by the app store you installed Threadory from (Google Play on Android, the App Store on iOS) under that store's own terms and privacy policy. We never see or receive your payment details, name or e-mail address. The app only asks the store whether Threadory Pro is owned on your store account, and remembers the answer on your device.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. Barcode scanning uses Google's ML Kit on the device (on Android through the barcode scanner module provided by Google Play services, on iOS as a library inside the app); barcode processing happens on the device and no images are sent anywhere.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, no social features and no external links that would be inappropriate for children.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally, you can delete all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated policy will be published at this address and summarized in the app's release notes before any change takes effect.
